How accurate is this?

This is single-view metrology, not lidar. Accuracy depends entirely on calibration and on how precisely you tap the corners.

  1. Photo mode — typically ±1–3% within about 3 m of the reference object. Use the biggest reference you can and keep the room corners near it.
  2. Scan mode — typically ±2–5%, dominated by the phone-height and focal-length calibration and by compass drift.
  3. Best practice — tape-measure one or two walls, type those lengths into the plan and lock them, then hit Square up. The rest of the room is scaled and squared to the measurements you trust.
